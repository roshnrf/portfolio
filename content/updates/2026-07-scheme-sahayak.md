A real-time voice pipeline that answers questions about government schemes and refuses to guess when retrieval confidence is low. Median response time is about 1.0 to 1.5 seconds without a GPU.

The hard part was audio, not the model: the assistant kept hearing itself and replying. [Read the write-up](/projects/scheme-sahayak).
